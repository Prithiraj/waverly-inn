A room with a memory

Old New York without the museum glass.

The building is a 19th-century townhouse; the restaurant traces its life as Ye Waverly Inn & Garden to the 1920s. The present-era Waverly Inn reopened in 2006 and has kept the house's fireplaces, close quarters and sense of discretion central to the experience.
