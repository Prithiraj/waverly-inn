## Preferred production replacement order

1. Ask the restaurant for its approved original photo archive and logo files.
2. Confirm digital marketing / website rights for any commissioned photographer work.
3. Keep the same crop intent and scene categories where possible: night exterior, mural room, conservatory, fireplace/bar, pot pie, burger, Bank Street context.
4. Only retain editorial imagery if an explicit license covers the intended commercial website use.

## Current owner-controlled media

The existing official homepage exposes `https://www.waverlynyc.com/image001.jpg`, a Waverly Inn & Garden wordmark image. The prototype currently recreates the name typographically rather than hotlinking that raster logo, pending receipt of a proper brand asset.
